## Forced Discount Rate (cut from CV and site, Sept 2026)

With Simon Levin I am developing a related framework for resource use: that structural conditions can raise the effective rate at which people discount the future, so that liquidating a resource becomes the rational choice rather than a failure of foresight.

## Old About "Technical" section (pre-Sept 2026 site)

Most of my mathematical work has focused on a deceptively simple question: how do agents — animals, people, groups — gather information and make decisions in environments that are complex and constantly changing? My PhD developed new frameworks for this problem, and my postdoctoral work extended it to human social networks, where I studied how structural biases in communication (who talks to whom, what gets shared) can drive polarization and systematic error even among people trying their best to get things right.
